## Changing an Order After Placement

Customers can change their delivery address within 1 hour of placing an order, by going 
to Order History > Edit Order. After 1 hour, the order enters processing and the address 
can no longer be changed by the customer directly.

If a customer needs an address change after the 1-hour window, a support agent can 
manually update it as long as the order has not yet shipped. Once shipped, no changes 
are possible, and the customer must be advised to contact the courier directly or 
refuse delivery.
