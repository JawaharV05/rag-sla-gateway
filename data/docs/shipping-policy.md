## Shipping Policy

Domestic orders typically arrive within 3-5 business days. International orders take 
7-14 business days and may be subject to customs fees, which are the responsibility 
of the customer.

Tracking information is emailed automatically once the order ships. Delays due to 
customs holds are outside of our control and are not eligible for shipping refunds, 
though the order itself remains eligible for standard return/refund once received.
