## Refund Policy

Standard refunds are processed for items returned within 30 days of delivery, in original 
condition. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days 
after the returned item is received and inspected.

### Exceptions for Damaged Goods
If an item arrives damaged, customers have up to 60 days to report the issue, not the 
standard 30. Damaged goods reported after 30 days but within 60 days require photo 
evidence and manager approval before the refund is processed.
